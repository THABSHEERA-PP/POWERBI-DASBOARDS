The Healthcare Analytics Dashboard created using Power BI provides a clear overview of hospital performance and patient details. It shows important information such as the total number of patients, average waiting time, and patient satisfaction score, helping to understand how well the hospital is functioning. The dashboard also displays patient admission status, showing nearly equal numbers of admitted and non-admitted patients. It analyzes patients based on age group, gender, and race to understand patient demographics. Additionally, it shows which departments patients are referred to, such as general practice or orthopedics. The report also highlights how many patients are seen within 30 minutes, helping measure service efficiency. Furthermore, it includes data on patient visits by day and hour to identify busy times in the hospital. Overall, this dashboard helps in improving healthcare services by providing insights into patient flow, waiting time, and overall hospital performance.
