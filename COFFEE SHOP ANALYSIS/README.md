The Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard created in Power BI shows a simple overview of how a coffee shop is performing. It displays total sales, number of orders, and quantity of items sold, helping us understand the overall business performance. The dashboard also compares sales between weekdays and weekends, showing that more sales happen during weekdays. It highlights different store locations to identify which branch earns more revenue. Additionally, it shows daily sales trends to track busy and slow days, and breaks down sales by product categories like coffee, tea, and bakery items, where coffee has the highest sales. It also lists the top-selling products and analyzes sales by hour and day to find peak business times. Overall, this dashboard helps in making better decisions by clearly showing sales patterns, customer behavior, and product performance.
